ABOUT LYTON MUPANGA

A multidisciplinary mind building a life around useful work.

THE STORY

Learn deeply. Create boldly. Lead intentionally.

Lyton Mupanga is a creator, entrepreneur, academic tutor, public speaker and strategist pursuing a multidisciplinary path at the intersection of engineering, education, media and business.

His work spans personal development, digital content, creative production, entrepreneurship, learning and community-building. Rather than treating these interests as separate identities, the goal is to build an ecosystem where each capability strengthens the others.

He is currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Technology in Chemical Engineering at KIIT University while continuing to develop brands, media projects and creative ventures.
